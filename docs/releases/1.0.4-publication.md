## Security and limits

The exact published-image Trivy all-severity scan found **49 vulnerability
occurrences**, **15 distinct CVEs** and **19 package/CVE pairs**: zero Critical,
21 High, 23 Medium and five Low, with zero detected image secret matches.
ImageSharp 3.1.12 retains three distinct High advisories repeated across seven
manifests and five distinct CVEs overall. Its previously verified Release
upgrade license-key constraint remains; no new dependency patch or licensing
experiment is claimed. Owner configuration volumes are outside the image scan,
and zero detected secrets does not prove the absence of every secret.

The frozen-source scan has an exact **46-file Git witness** against `fee14c10…`.
Semgrep ran 27 C# rules with zero parse errors and retained two inherited
calendar INFO authorization findings, triaged against authenticated fallback
policy/API-key middleware. It is not a whole-repository audit or a dedicated
live calendar-request test. The frontend audit's three High `braces` occurrences
remain in build dependencies outside the runtime image. No clean-security claim
is made.

Provider fixtures establish exercised contracts, not external uptime, live
private-account writes, premium SIMKL behavior or real indexer downloads. Remote
account edits can race with an already-running advance; providers do not supply
cross-service atomic compare-and-set. Tanukarr sends progress/history changes
without status/rating/custom-list edits; a provider may apply its own rules.
